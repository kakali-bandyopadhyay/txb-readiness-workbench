Measure definitions
  • Readiness = mandatory actions with recorded owner completion and evidence, or an accepted exception, divided by all mandatory actions.
  • Overdue = incomplete actions whose due date is before today.
  • Due soon = incomplete actions due today through five days from today, inclusive.
  • Defect age = elapsed whole days since the recorded open timestamp.
  • Team readiness is an illustrative count of impacted-owner action completion, mapped to impacted team owners.
  • Region filter reflects release regions; seeded readiness records are synthetic and are not region-specific.

All counts and dates are illustrative. No operational effect is claimed.
